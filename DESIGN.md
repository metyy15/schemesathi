## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the progressive convergence of modern Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) and high-assurance GovTech SaaS. It balances state-grade authority, radical functional clarity, and human warmth. Designed for citizens, policy analysts, administrators, and enterprise compliance leads, the interface evokes sovereign trust, frictionless delivery, and civic pride without archaic bureaucratic clutter.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Minimalist Sovereign Utility**:
- **Authoritative & Crisp**: Precision metrics, monoline civic badges, structured tabular layouts, and clear typographic hierarchy inspire unshakeable institutional reliability.
- **Human & Dignified**: Intentional warm undertones, generous breathing room, and contextual microcopy treat every interaction as an empowering public service rather than an audit.
- **DPI-Centric Minimalism**: Highly scannable UI cards, subtle 1px divider structures, and calibrated accent spots (Saffron focus, Emerald verification) guide users through high-stakes multi-step discovery, eligibility checking, and direct benefit disbursement.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high visual contrast, semantic clarity, and institutional prestige.

### Surface & Canvas
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: A calm, architectural off-white that eliminates screen glare while maintaining crispness.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for focal cards, modals, sheets, and active form modules.
- **Subtle Ash Dividers (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-frequency border tone for structural segmentation, keeping content separation clean without visual noise.
- **Muted Surface (`#F1F5F9`)**: Form control backdrops, tabular striping, and inactive state containers.

### Sovereign Navy (Primary Authority)
- **Deep Navy Base (`#0F172A`)**: The dominant structural color used for primary CTAs, main titles, top-level navigation shells, and high-impact data anchors.
- **Slate Secondary (`#1E293B`)**: Employed for subtitles, active states, nested headings, and emphasized metadata.
- **Slate Muted (`#64748B`)**: Used for labels, secondary breadcrumbs, and standard metadata keys.

### Civic Accents
- **Bharat Saffron Accent (`#E05A12` / `#EA580C`)**: High-priority interactive triggers, flagship application actions, urgent alerts, and critical verification milestones.
- **Emerald Accent (`#047857` / `#10B981`)**: The sovereign verification green. Used for direct benefit transfer (DBT) statuses, successful eligibility validations, scheme sanctions, and trustworthy state seals.

## Typography

The pairing between **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** bridges modern tech elegance with bureaucratic rigor.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Hero Figures)**: Offers sculpted, contemporary geometric letterforms that feel welcoming yet unmistakably institutional. Used for high-level page headers, numerical metric values, and card headlines.
- **Inter (Body, Tables, Controls, and Labels)**: Delivers unmatched neutrality, structural spacing, and legibility down to 11px micro-copy. Used for all contextual documentation, multi-field applications, scheme eligibility matrices, and badge text.
- **Tabular Numerals**: In data-heavy views (direct benefit transfers, fiscal quotas, Aadhaar/PAN verifications), apply `font-feature-settings: 'tnum'` to guarantee vertical alignment of numeric data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an 8-point geometric spatial scale within a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid.

### Layout Principles
- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered, `2rem` margin, `1.5rem` gutter.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, `1.5rem` margin, `1.25rem` gutter.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `1rem` margin, `1rem` gutter.
- **Spatial Rhythm**:
  - `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-label pairing, compact pill paddings, and tag gutters.
  - `space-md` (16px): Input field interior padding, standard card stack spacing, table cell heights.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Card interior paddings, section headers to block content separation.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Inter-module section gaps, portal dashboard canvas stacks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Boundary Architecture**: an intentional balance of sharp 1px ash borders (`#E2E8F0`) augmented by ultra-diffused, cool-tinted ambient shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Hex `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation. Foundational backing for layout surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules)**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), solid `1px border: #E2E8F0`, ambient shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered / Interactive Cards)**: `1px border: #CBD5E1`, ambient shadow `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`. Transform upward by `1px` on non-touch devices.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Dropdowns)**: Solid `1px border: #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (System Dialogs & Modals)**: Backed by a neutral overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur), crisp `1px border: #E2E8F0`, deep diffused lift `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy employs controlled, modern corner radii that keep the experience friendly without compromising institutional precision:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard form controls, input elements, buttons, utility cards, and table containers.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: High-level overview cards, hero scheme banners, multi-step stepper frames, and modal dialogues.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Large marketing wrappers or civic onboarding cards.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: System tags, scheme eligibility chips, badge statuses, and micro-toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign**: Solid `#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `1px solid transparent`. Hover shifts to `#1E293B`. Active compresses to scale `0.99`.
- **Saffron Action**: `#E05A12` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius. Reserved for high-intent actions like "Apply Now", "Check Eligibility", or "Verify Aadhaar". Hover transitions to `#EA580C`.
- **Secondary Outlined**: Solid `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Verified Status Badge**: Pill radius, background `#ECFDF5`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, text `#047857`, font `label-sm` with optional leading 6px emerald status dot.
- **Pending/Action Required Badge**: Pill radius, background `#FFF7ED`, border `1px solid #FED7AA`, text `#C2410C`.
- **Ministry / Sector Chip**: Rounded `0.375rem`, background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. Interactive variations include a hover ring of `#0F172A`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Height `44px` for optimal touch and desktop ergonomics.
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State**: `1px solid #0F172A` with a distinct `3px` focus ring in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Error State**: `1px solid #DC2626` with `3px` ring in `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.12)`.

### Cards & Scheme Containers
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `0.75rem` or `1rem` radius.
- Padding: `1.5rem` desktop, `1rem` mobile.
- Structured header housing: Left-aligned Ministry logo/monogram and Title; right-aligned Benefit Tag (e.g., "₹6,000 / yr" or "Direct Transfer").
- Bottom utility bar divided by a subtle `1px solid #F1F5F9` border separator for deadline countdowns and criteria icons.

### Verification / Checklists & Radio Tiles
- Radio and checkbox targets maintain a clear `20px` bounding box with `1.5px` border in `#94A3B8`.
- Selected checkbox: `#0F172A` fill with white checkmark.
- Interactive criteria cards: Border highlights to `#0F172A` on selection with a subtle `#F8FAFC` tinted wash.

### Steppers & Trackers (DPI Lifecycle)
- Horizontal step sequences featuring numerical indicators: Inactive `#E2E8F0` circle with `#64748B` text; Active `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text; Completed `#047857` with white checkmark.
- Connecting lines: `2px solid #E2E8F0` transitioning smoothly to `#047857` on stage completion.
